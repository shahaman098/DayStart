DayStart Privacy Policy

App: DayStart (com.loudwake.app)
Last updated: 23 July 2026

DayStart is an Android alarm clock app. Its core purpose is to schedule wake-up alarms, present wake challenges, and help you stay awake after the alarm. This policy explains what the app accesses on your device and how that information is handled.

1. Summary

  • Alarm settings and wake sessions are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate accounts, analytics SDKs, ad networks, or our own cloud backends for this app.
  • Optional Voice set uses your device’s speech recognition (often provided by Google). That audio may be processed by Google’s speech services according to Google’s terms — DayStart does not upload recordings to a DayStart server.
  • Camera photos used for optional Wake Gate challenges are processed on-device with Google ML Kit and are never uploaded by DayStart.

2. Data the app stores on your device

DayStart stores locally (using Android SharedPreferences and temporary files):

  • Alarm times, names, repeat rules, sound choices, voice-coach settings, and wake-mode settings
  • Whether an alarm is enabled
  • Current wake / Relapse Guard session state
  • Temporary photo files used only for on-device challenge verification

This data stays on your device unless you back up your phone with your own backup tools.

3. Permissions and why they are requested

  • Exact alarms — required so wake-up alarms fire at the scheduled time. DayStart is an alarm/clock app.
  • Notifications & full-screen intent — to show the alarm and wake UI even when the phone is locked or another app is open.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — to play the alarm tone while audio is actively playing during a wake session.
  • Vibrate / modify audio / Do Not Disturb access — so the alarm can be heard and felt as configured.
  • Boot completed — to reschedule alarms after reboot or app update.
  • Battery optimization exemption (optional, user-approved) — Android may delay or kill background work. Allowing unrestricted battery helps alarms and Relapse Guard remain reliable. You can decline.
  • Microphone — only for optional Voice set (creating alarms by speaking). Speech is handled by the system speech recognizer; DayStart does not keep a cloud copy of your voice on its own servers.
  • Camera — only for optional Wake Gate photo challenges. Photos are analyzed on-device and are not sent to us.
  • Activity recognition / physical activity — optional movement-based wake challenges; stays on-device.

4. Photos and on-device processing

If you use a camera challenge, DayStart may capture a photo and run on-device image labeling (Google ML Kit) to check whether the scene matches the challenge. The model runs locally. Photos are not uploaded by DayStart, not shared by us, and not used for advertising. Temporary image files may be created for verification and can be cleared with app data.

5. Voice set and speech recognition

If you use Voice set, DayStart starts the Android speech recognition UI so you can dictate alarm dates and times. Recognition is performed by the device / Google speech services (depending on your phone and settings). DayStart uses the resulting text locally to create alarms. We do not operate a DayStart speech server.

6. Data we do not collect

  • No account registration
  • No email, name, or contact list collection by DayStart
  • No location tracking for advertising
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party advertising SDKs in the app

7. Children

DayStart is a general-audience alarm utility. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the camera challenges to photograph other people without their permission.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny Microphone, Camera, or Activity permissions; related features will not work until granted.
  • You can keep battery optimization enabled; alarm reliability may vary by manufacturer.
  • You can clear app storage or uninstall DayStart to remove local data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page and, when required, note the change in the Play Store listing.
